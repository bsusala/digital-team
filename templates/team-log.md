# Team log

Append-only ledger of the team, written by the pilot. One entry per event, newest at the bottom. Corrections
are new entries naming what they correct; nothing is edited in place. A gap is left visible, never backfilled.

Every entry opens with a UTC instant generated by the command that writes it, and carries a class:

- **declaration** — the operator said something that binds (quote the words, name the session)
- **record** — something happened (a relay, a fix, a restart), with the evidence
- **receipt** — something was received or forwarded: from whom, to whom, when
